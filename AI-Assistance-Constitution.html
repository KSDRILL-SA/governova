Safe Actions

Auto-Allowed: No Requires Approval: Yes
LEVEL 4
Critical Changes
Modify schema, delete data, change auth, install deps
Auto-Allowed: No Allowed: NEVER
Hard Block Principle: L4 isn't just restricted — it's allowed: false in the permission matrix. Not a warning, not a confirmation dialog — a hard block coded into the permission layer itself.

Canonical AI Governance Answers
The 8 Official Answers — When Any Constitution Touches AI, Defer Here
QuestionAnswer
How much should AI be allowed to do autonomously?Four-level hard boundary model. L4 is allowed: false — a hard block, not a warning.
When can AI act without approval?Never on ambiguity. Confidence <70% routes to human. "Ask when uncertain" is the rule.
How do you prevent AI from improvising?Match a runbook before any action. If no runbook exists, halt and alert — never invent a path.
What actions can AI take on critical state?None. Reversibility is a required field. Any irreversible action is L4 (hard blocked).
How does the system get smarter over time?Not retraining — structured documentation. Every incident produces a document that updates runbooks.
How severe are security violations?Never "medium severity" — immediate hard stops with no AI recovery autonomy.
What makes an alert actionable?A pre-filled decision packet. Human should act without investigating first.
What can AI never do?The MUST NEVER list: act blindly, hide uncertainty, expand scope, irreversible actions, override rules, improvise, L4 actions.
