§ Part 1 — Severity Levels (IR1-IR5)
Purpose

Classify incidents by impact for appropriate response.

PART 1 / IR1-IR5Severity Levels2 Critical · 1 High · 2 Medium
IR1SEV0 — Critical (5 min response)Critical

Complete system outage, data loss, security breach. Response within 5 minutes. Page on-call, all hands if needed.

IR2SEV1 — High (15 min response)Critical

Major feature broken, significant performance degradation, partial outage affecting >10% of users. Response within 15 minutes.

IR3SEV2 — Medium (4 hour response)High

Non-critical feature broken, minor performance issue, UI bug affecting <10% of users. Response within 4 hours.

IR4SEV3 — Low (1 week response)Medium

Cosmetic issue, typo, non-blocking bug. Response within 1 week.

IR5SEV4 — Suggestion (next sprint)Medium

Enhancement request, nice-to-have. Response during next sprint planning.

§ Part 2 — Detection & Triage (IR6-IR10)
Purpose

Find incidents before users do, triage quickly.

PART 2 / IR6-IR10Detection & Triage2 Critical · 2 High · 1 Medium
IR6Automated Detection FirstHigh

Incidents MUST be detected via automated monitoring first (not user reports). User reports are a failure of monitoring.

IR7Runbook per AlertHigh

Every alert MUST have a clear runbook link documented in alert configuration.

IR8False Positive ReviewMedium

False positives MUST be reviewed weekly. Adjust thresholds or silence noisy alerts.

IR9Triage Within Response TimeCritical

Incident triage (severity assignment, IC named) MUST happen within severity response time.

IR10RCA Within 24 Hours (CLARIFIED)Critical

Service restoration is immediate priority. Resolution = users restored. Root Cause Analysis MUST be completed within 24 hours of resolution. Incident state remains "Monitoring" until service stable.

⚡
v1.1 CLARIFIED: Resolution = service restored. RCA = within 24h post-resolution.
§ Part 3 — Response Roles & Process (IR11-IR16)
Purpose

Clear roles, blameless process, effective coordination.

PART 3 / IR11-IR16Response Roles & Process4 Critical · 2 High
IR11Incident Commander RequiredCritical

Incident Commander (IC) role MUST be assigned for SEV0 and SEV1 incidents.

IR12IC ResponsibilitiesCritical

IC is responsible for: communication, timeline management, decision-making, resolution coordination, stakeholder updates.

IR13IC Does NOT Fix (Team Context)High

In team contexts (2+ responders): IC is NOT responsible for debugging, fixing, or writing code. IC coordinates; others execute. See IR37 for solo founder exception.

IR14Scribe Required for SEV0/SEV1High

Scribe role MUST be assigned for SEV0 and SEV1 incidents (timeline documentation).

IR15Timeline RecordedCritical

Timeline MUST be recorded: detection → triage → mitigation → resolution → recovery.

IR16Blameless CultureCritical

Blameless culture: Incidents are system failures, not people failures. Post-mortems MUST NOT assign blame to individuals.

§ Part 4 — Communication (IR17-IR21)
Purpose

Keep everyone informed, reduce confusion.

PART 4 / IR17-IR21Communication4 High · 1 Medium
IR17Internal Communication ChannelHigh

Internal communication channel (Slack #incidents) MUST be used for SEV0 and SEV1 incidents.

IR18Status Page for SEV0High

Status page updates REQUIRED for SEV0 incidents within 15 minutes of declaration.

IR19Customer Communication ThresholdHigh

Customer communication REQUIRED for SEV0 incidents or any incident affecting >10% of users.

IR20Communication TemplatesMedium

Communication template MUST be used for consistent incident updates.

IR21Update FrequencyHigh

Updates MUST be posted every 30 minutes for SEV0, every 60 minutes for SEV1 (even if "no change, still investigating").

§ Part 5 — Post-Mortems & Learning (IR22-IR27)
Purpose

Learn from incidents, prevent recurrence.

PART 5 / IR22-IR27Post-Mortems & Learning4 Critical · 2 High
IR22Post-Mortem RequiredCritical

Post-mortem REQUIRED for all SEV0 and SEV1 incidents.

IR23Post-Mortem TimelineHigh

Post-mortem MUST be completed within 5 business days of incident resolution.

IR24Post-Mortem Content (ENHANCED)Critical

Post-mortem MUST include: Timeline, Root Cause, Impact, What Went Well, What Went Wrong, Action Items, Lessons Learned.

📝
v1.1 ENHANCED: Added "What Went Well" section for blameless positive reinforcement.
IR25Action Items with OwnersCritical

Action items MUST have owners and due dates.

IR26Action Items Tracked to CompletionHigh

Action items MUST be tracked to completion (no orphaned items).

IR27Post-Mortem Shared with TeamCritical

Post-mortem MUST be shared with entire team (blameless, learning-focused).

§ Part 6 — Runbooks & Playbooks (IR28-IR31)
Purpose

Documented, tested procedures for common incidents.

PART 6 / IR28-IR31Runbooks & Playbooks3 High · 1 Medium
IR28Runbook per Critical AlertHigh

Runbook MUST exist for every critical alert (SEV0/SEV1 potential).

IR29Runbook ContentHigh

Runbook MUST include: alert meaning, severity, diagnostic steps, mitigation steps, escalation path.

IR30Runbook Testing QuarterlyMedium

Runbooks MUST be tested quarterly (simulate alert, follow runbook, time it).

IR31Runbook Version ControlHigh

Runbooks MUST be version controlled (same repository as code).

§ Part 7 — On-Call Rotation (IR32-IR36)
Purpose

24/7 coverage, clear expectations, prevent burnout.

PART 7 / IR32-IR36On-Call Rotation1 Critical · 3 High · 1 Medium
IR32On-Call Rotation RequiredCritical

On-call rotation MUST exist for SEV0 and SEV1 incidents.

IR33On-Call Boundaries (Solo Founder)High

For solo founder: on-call boundaries MUST be documented. Response: within 30 minutes 9 AM-9 PM, within 2 hours 9 PM-9 AM.

IR34On-Call HandoverMedium

On-call handover MUST include: open incidents, recent changes, known issues, runbook updates.

IR35Post-On-Call ReportHigh

Post-on-call report MUST be shared (alerts received, incidents handled, improvements needed) — 5 minutes max.

IR36On-Call CompensationHigh

On-call compensation: time-off-in-lieu (1 hour off for 1 hour on-call worked).

§ Part 8 — Solo Operations & Fatigue Prevention (IR37-IR40) 🆕
Purpose

Adapt enterprise practices for solo founders/small teams. Prevent burnout.

PART 8 / IR37-IR40Solo Operations & Fatigue Prevention2 Critical · 2 High
IR37SEV0 Solo Protocol 🆕Critical

Solo Operator Exception: When a single person is both IC and Responder, IR13 is suspended. Solo operator MAY fix code directly, but MUST preserve timeline by vocalizing actions aloud for recording or typing actions into incident channel as they go.

🆕
v1.1 NEW: Solo founder may fix code directly but must log actions.
IR38Minimum Viable Coverage 🆕High

On-call rotation MUST have either: (a) minimum 2 engineers in rotation, OR (b) documented solo boundaries with customer-facing SLA. "Single point of failure" is acceptable only with documented SLA boundaries.

🆕
v1.1 NEW: Ensures solo coverage is intentional, not accidental.
IR39Incident Fatigue Monitoring 🆕Critical

Fatigue Threshold: >3 pages in 24 hours, OR >10 pages in 7 days, triggers an automatic review. Review outcome MUST be one of: adjust thresholds, fix underlying issue, add secondary on-call, or documented acceptance of alert volume.

🆕
v1.1 NEW: Prevents burnout and alert fatigue.
IR40Tooling Over Memory 🆕High

Compliance with this constitution SHOULD be automated via tooling, not human memory. Humans panic during incidents. Tooling (PagerDuty, Slack workflows, status page integrations) enforces rules automatically.

🆕
v1.1 NEW: Automate compliance, don't rely on memory under stress.
